# Contributing

Design discussion, issue reports, threat models, portability observations, and reproducible test findings are welcome after the repository opens. Please distinguish direct evidence, interpretation, proposal, and uncertainty, and do not include private prompts, credentials, personal data, or material you lack permission to share.

Pull requests are temporarily held pending adoption of contributor terms compatible with the noncommercial public license and possible separate commercial licensing. Opening a pull request does not grant the maintainers additional relicensing rights or guarantee acceptance.

No contribution should claim live Hermes, Pi, or Exocore compatibility without a named version, direct test method, evidence, and limitations.

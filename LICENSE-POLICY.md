## File-level precedence

An explicit file-level notice controls that file. Otherwise:

- material under `docs/`, plus `README.md`, uses the documentation license;
- all other included material uses the root PolyForm Noncommercial license;
- third-party material retains its identified license;
- absent or ambiguous third-party rights fail closed and the affected material must not be redistributed.

Moving or copying content between license classes does not silently relicense it. A generated workspace receives the software license unless an exact generated artifact says otherwise.

## Commercial and contributor rights

The public grants are non-exclusive, so the copyright holder may offer separate commercial licenses. External code or documentation contributions remain held until contributor terms preserve the rights needed for this distribution and any commercial license. Do not infer relicensing permission from a pull request alone.

## Protection boundary

Licensing governs copyrightable expression actually released. It does not make an underlying idea, system, method, process, algorithm, or operational concept exclusively owned. Protection of the deeper system therefore depends on preserving the public/private source boundary, maintaining reasonable secrecy over retained material, and obtaining qualified advice before disclosing potentially patentable or trade-secret material.
